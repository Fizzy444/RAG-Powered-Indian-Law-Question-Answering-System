Common Questions

Is the legal advice binding?

No. Legal AI provides information and research assistance based on Indian law. It is a tool for professionals and not a substitute for a registered advocate.

Is my data secure?

Absolutely. We use end-to-end encryption for all uploaded documents and chat logs. We do not use your private data to train our public models.

What laws are covered?

We cover the Constitution of India, BNS/BNSS (new Sanhitas), IPC, CrPC, CPC, Evidence Act, Corporate Law, and major Taxation laws — all updated to 2026.

Who is this built for?

Advocates, law students, judges' researchers, legal aid workers, in-house counsel, and anyone who needs to understand Indian law quickly and accurately.

Can I upload my own documents?

Yes. You can upload PDFs and Word documents. The assistant will analyse the content, flag key clauses, and find relevant statutes and case law automatically.

How current is the data?

Our index is updated continuously. The BNS and BNSS changes effective 2024–2026 are already reflected, including the latest gazette notifications.
